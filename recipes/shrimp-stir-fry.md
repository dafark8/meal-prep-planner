tags: ["entrees"]

A fast, colorful stir fry with tender shrimp and crisp vegetables over steamed rice.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 cup snap peas
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 cups cooked white rice
