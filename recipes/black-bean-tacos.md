Smoky spiced black bean tacos with creamy avocado and crunchy cabbage.

## Ingredients

- 2 cans black beans, drained
- 8 corn tortillas
- 2 avocados, sliced
- 1 cup fresh salsa
- 2 cups shredded cabbage
- 2 limes, juiced
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- Sour cream, for serving
