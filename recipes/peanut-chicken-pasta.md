title: Peanut Chicken Pasta
tags: ["pasta"]
---

A quick and satisfying pasta dish with creamy peanut sauce and tender chicken over a bed of fresh spinach.

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts
- 8 oz pasta
- 3 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
