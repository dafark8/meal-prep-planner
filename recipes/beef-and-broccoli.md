title: Beef and Broccoli
tags: ["entrees"]
---

A takeout-style classic with tender sliced beef and crisp broccoli in a savory sauce.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb flank steak, thinly sliced
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 cups cooked white rice
