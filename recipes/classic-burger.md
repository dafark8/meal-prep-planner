A juicy, no-fuss classic burger with all the traditional fixings.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 4 burger buns
- 4 leaves romaine lettuce
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- 4 slices cheddar cheese
- 1 onion, sliced
- Dill pickles, sliced
- Ketchup
- Yellow mustard
