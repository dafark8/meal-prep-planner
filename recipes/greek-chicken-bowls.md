---
title: Greek Chicken Bowls
tags: ["entrees"]
---

Fresh Mediterranean-style bowls with herb-marinated chicken, crisp vegetables, and creamy tzatziki.

## Ingredients

- 4 chicken thighs, boneless
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 cup kalamata olives
- 1/2 cup feta cheese, crumbled
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup tzatziki
- 1 cup couscous
- 1 lemon, juiced
