A classic, silky tomato soup brightened with fresh basil and finished with a touch of cream.

## Ingredients

- 2 cans diced tomatoes
- 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cups vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
