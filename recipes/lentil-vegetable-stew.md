title: Lentil Vegetable Stew
tags: ["soups"]
---

A hearty, warming stew packed with plant-based protein and smoky spices.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup green lentils
- 3 carrots, diced
- 3 stalks celery, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 4 cups vegetable broth
